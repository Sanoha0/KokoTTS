How it works & privacy

KokoTTS runs Kokoro-82M on your device. The first run downloads model and runtime files from Hugging Face and a CDN. Those services receive ordinary network information, but your text and generated audio are processed locally. Downloads are cached when your browser permits it.

This version supports 28 American and British English voices. MP3 and WAV files are encoded locally. Text and audio clear when you close or reload the page.

Built with Kokoro, Transformers.js, and LAME.js. Open-source notices.
